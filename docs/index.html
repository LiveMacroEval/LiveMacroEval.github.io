Why a live benchmark?

Nowcasting is estimating an indicator before its official release. Central banks staff whole teams to do it.

  • LLM agents pair broad world knowledge with live web search.
  • They can be queried hourly. Institutional nowcasts update weekly at best.

The contamination problem. GDP and CPI are among the most reported numbers on earth, so their past values are almost certainly memorized in pretraining. Testing on past releases measures recall, not nowcasting.

LiveMacroEval scores only nowcasts made before the release. Contamination-free by construction — and it keeps running, which is why these numbers move.

The task

  • The window opens in the final week of the reference month.
  • It closes at the official release — weeks to months later.
  • The agent decides when to search, and what to search for.

    How we score

    Plain error on the released level ignores that indicators differ in economic weight. We use two market-grounded scores instead.

    Metric 1

    LiveMacro Score

    Does the nowcast explain how the market moved?

    • Macro surprises move equity prices.
    • We weight each indicator by its measured effect on S&P 500 futures at the release.
    • +1 perfect · 0 ties the consensus · −1 worse.

    Sixteen accuracies collapse into one comparable number.

    Metric 2

    LiveBetting Score

    If you traded the nowcast, would you make money?

    • Every hour before the release, bet $1 on the bucket holding the latest nowcast.
    • Prices are the crowd's odds, so liquidity is priced in.
    • Beating a confident crowd pays a lot; agreeing with it pays little.

    Unit-free, so it aggregates across very different indicators.

    Full definitions, estimation and caveats are in the paper.
