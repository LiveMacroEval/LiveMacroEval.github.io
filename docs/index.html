Can LLMs Take the Pulse of the Economy?

LLM agents nowcast sixteen U.S. headline macro indicators in real time. We score them against the equity market, against prediction markets, and against the professional economists who do this for a living.

01 — MotivationWhy a live benchmark

Nowcasting means estimating an indicator's value for the current period, before the official release. Central banks staff whole teams to do it.

LLM agents look like a natural fit for the job:

  • They pair broad world knowledge with real-time web search.
  • They can be queried hourly. Institutional nowcasts update weekly at best.

The contamination problem.

GDP and CPI are among the most widely reported numbers on earth. Their historical values are almost certainly memorized during pretraining.

Test a model on past releases and you measure recall, not nowcasting. The only clean test is a live one, where the answer does not yet exist.

LiveMacroEval runs that test. Every nowcast is made inside a window that closes at the scheduled release, so the target cannot be in the model's training data or in anything it retrieves. The benchmark is contamination-free by construction — and it keeps running, which is why the numbers on this page move.

02 — ProtocolThe task

For each of 16 U.S. headline indicators, an LLM agent with web search produces an hourly nowcast.

  • The window opens in the final week of the reference month.
  • The window closes at the official release — weeks to months later.
  • The agent decides for itself when to search, and what to search for.

    03 — MetricsHow we score

    Plain MSE on the released level has three problems. It treats every indicator as equally important. It confuses forecast quality with an indicator's scale. And it throws away the whole pre-release trajectory.

    So we use two market-grounded metrics instead.

    Metric 1

    LiveMacro Score

    Does the nowcast explain how the market actually moved?

    • Macro surprises — released value minus consensus — causally move equity prices.
    • We estimate each indicator's effect βi on E-mini S&P 500 futures in a tight [−5, +30] minute window around the release.
    • β is fit on a long historical sample and frozen before any live outcome is scored.
    • Every nowcast implies a surprise, and so an implied return.
    LiveMacro = (SScons − SSmodel) / (SScons + SSmodel)

    A bounded out-of-sample R². +1 perfectly anticipates announcement-window returns. 0 ties the Bloomberg consensus. −1 is arbitrarily worse. Because β weights each indicator by its measured market impact, sixteen accuracies collapse into one comparable number.

    Metric 2

    LiveBetting Score

    If you traded the nowcast, would you make money?

    • Each Polymarket-style macro market is a set of numeric buckets trading as binary contracts.
    • Every hour before the release, we bet $1 on the bucket holding the agent's latest nowcast.
    • That buys 1/p shares at the prevailing market price p.

    Because p is the crowd's implied probability, liquidity is priced in by construction. Agree with a confident crowd and winning pays little. Beat a wrong crowd and it pays a lot. The metric rewards beating the crowd, and it is unit-free, so it aggregates across very different indicators. A single GPT-5 GDP window can hold over 1,400 hourly bets.

    05 — FindingsResults

    Six months of live evaluation. The top agent ties the professional consensus overall — but that aggregate hides sharp differences across the economy.

    Headline: a tie at the top

    By theme: where the skill is

    Splitting the score by theme shows the aggregate is an average of very different stories.

    • Above consensus — Supply and Production, Housing. Both rest on higher-frequency components, such as mortgage applications and housing sentiment. Updating inside the window pays off.
    • Below consensus — Demand and Inflation. Well-anchored inflation expectations protect the professional consensus on headline inflation.
    • Flat — Labor Market. Every model sits near zero, consistent with a signal-to-noise ceiling on the household survey.

    LiveBetting: trading against the Fed

    • Real GDP. Four agents finish the window with positive cumulative returns. Three beat the Atlanta Fed. Only the New York Fed stays ahead.
    • Unemployment rate. The leading agent finishes above the Chicago Fed.
    • CPI. The Cleveland Fed and the Bloomberg consensus still beat every agent — a real limit where institutional models are tuned to known economic structure.

    Case study: watching a belief update

    An hourly cadence lets us trace, after the fact, how one piece of news enters an agent's information set.

    • GPT-5's March 2026 CPI nowcast sits near 2.7% through early April.
    • On April 8 it jumps to about 3% inside a tight intraday window, then tracks the realized print.
    • Two scheduled events fall in that window: the EIA Petroleum Status Report, showing a large gasoline-inventory drawdown, and FOMC minutes flagging upside inflation risk.
    • Both push the same way. The size of the revision matches a back-of-the-envelope gasoline contribution under BLS expenditure weights.
